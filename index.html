About Green Life Marathon 2026

Organized by Pasumai Ingarangal in collaboration with Sugan Suga Hospital, the Green Life Marathon 2026 is a grand fitness and environmental awareness event held in celebration of Children's Day. Designed to inspire the younger generation and adults alike, this event emphasizes wellness, environmental protection, and community health. Join us on November 15, 2026, to run for a greener, healthier future!
