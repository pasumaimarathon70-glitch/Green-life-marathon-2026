About The Event

The Green Life Marathon 2026 is a premier Children's Day Special Edition sporting event organized by Pasumai Ingarangal. Designed to foster community wellness and athletic enthusiasm, this grand run connects thousands of passionate runners dedicated to health, endurance, and environmental sustainability.

Through this athletic initiative, Pasumai Ingarangal aims to drive meaningful green initiatives and ecological awareness across Tirupur. Every step taken on race day contributes directly towards building a cleaner, greener, and healthier planet for future generations.
